# 1.13: the full reviews

Malinois 1.13 had three full reviews. Each looked at the release candidate that was about to be archived. The three
candidates were successive builds in the private repository, 1.13 (88) twice and then 1.13 (89), so their commits are
not in this one. The build submitted to the App Store, 1.13 (90), is the third candidate with the fixes taken after the
third review. Between reviews, the findings taken into 1.13 were fixed, those in the app each with a test that fails
without the fix, and gpt6-astra, a model that runs in Codex, looked at each round of fixes, in a shorter review of the
changes, before the next step.

**How to read the tables of findings.** Each has one row per finding; a finding that several reports raised has one row
that names them all. The severity is the reviewer's own, and some reviewers did not rate every finding. "Fixed in 1.13"
means the fix is in 1.13 (90), the build submitted to the App Store. "Planned for a later release" means the work is
recorded for a release after 1.13. "As designed" means the behaviour is intended and described in SECURITY.md or a
decision record (`docs/adr`). Where a finding is left out of its report until its fix ships, its row says so; one of
1.13's was, in the third review. The reports name files and lines of the candidate they read, so line numbers in the earlier reports may have
moved since; some files they name (the store texts, the release tools, the private repository's README) are not in this
repository. Where a report linked a file to its copy on the reviewer's Mac, the link was taken out with the Mac's other
details, and its text left as written. One report also named the developer team ID the app is signed with;
it is taken out too. Reviewers are named as their reports name them: the first review's Codex report
gives the program it ran in, and the later reports from that program give their model, gpt6-astra.
